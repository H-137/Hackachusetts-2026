# Synthetic demo data

Everything in this directory is **synthetic**. None of it is a real genome or a
real person's data. It exists to develop and demonstrate the explorer fully
offline (HACKATHON_PROJECT_PLAN.md Section 11).

## Files

Parser fixtures (Step 1):

- `parser_example_four_rows.txt` — the four-row example from the plan. Parses
  cleanly but is **insufficient for inference** by design.
- `synthetic_valid.txt` — a small well-formed file.
- `no_calls.txt`, `conflicting_duplicates.txt`, `malformed_records.txt`,
  `mixed_line_endings.txt`, `unsupported_chromosomes.txt`, `indels.txt` — edge
  cases exercised by the parser tests.

Precomputed analysis bundle (Steps 2–6, assumed complete):

- `analysis_bundle.json` — synthetic normalized outputs (ancestry segments,
  variant observations, frequency annotations, disease evidence, disease
  scores) that the integrated explorer renders. Generated by
  `scripts/make_analysis_bundle.py`. Clearly labeled `synthetic: true`.

  **Demo individual** (designed to be realistic AND to exercise the whole
  dashboard): a recently-admixed person of primarily West African ancestry with
  substantial Ashkenazi Jewish and some other European ancestry (~57% African,
  ~26% Ashkenazi Jewish, ~17% European by span). Local ancestry is drawn from a
  small palette (1000G population codes + AJ) as a few LONG contiguous segments
  per chromosome copy — not a random patchwork. It surfaces several stories at
  once: an OBSERVED HBB HbS carrier (sickle cell trait) over African ancestry;
  Ashkenazi founder variants (BRCA1/BRCA2/HEXA) that are NOT ASSAYED but have AJ
  ancestry overlaid (the "ancestry supports attention, array can't confirm"
  case); a CONFLICTING-interpretation BRCA2 SNP kept visible; and a cleanly
  assayed ARSA SNP with the clinical allele absent. All fabricated-for-demo.

> The bundle is illustrative. Segment ancestry calls, probabilities, and the
> disease score are fabricated-for-demo values, not inference outputs. They must
> never be presented as a real result.
